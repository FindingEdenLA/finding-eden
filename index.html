Why Finding Eden Exists

In 2019, I reached one of the lowest points in my life. I felt exhausted, overwhelmed, and unsure of what my purpose was anymore. During that time I realized something simple. When someone feels like they have no one — when the weight of the world is sitting on their shoulders and in their heart — what they need most isn’t advice. They need a place to rest. A place where they can breathe. A place where they can feel safe enough to exist without pressure. That realization became the seed for Finding Eden. Finding Eden exists to create a community-centered space where people can reconnect with themselves and others through storytelling, creativity, movement, and supportive community. This vision began with my own healing journey, but it is meant to belong to everyone who has ever needed a place to breathe again.

What You'll Find Here

  • Storytelling and emotional healing
  • Creative and somatic workshops
  • Supportive community gatherings
  • Tools for self reflection and recovery
